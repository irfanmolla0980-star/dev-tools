📖 About This Tool

What is it?

Lorem Ipsum Generator creates dummy text for testing layouts, prototypes, and designs. It can also replace your real text with Lorem — and restore it back.

What is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem Ipsum is dummy Latin text used since the 1500s. It has a normal-looking word distribution, so designers use it to test how real content will look without being distracted by readable words.

Two Modes

  • ⚡ Generate: Create Lorem Ipsum or custom words — as paragraphs, sentences, words, or characters
  • 🔄 Replace: Turn your real text into Lorem — and restore it back with one click

Options

  • Start with "Lorem ipsum..." — classic opening
  • Wrap in <p> tags — ready for HTML
  • UPPERCASE — all caps output
  • Bullet points — list format

How to use?

  1. Pick Generate or Replace
  2. Choose type and quantity (or paste your text)
  3. Adjust options if needed
  4. Click ⚡ Generate or 🔄 Loremify
  5. Copy or download the output

Tips

  • Use Custom Words to generate text from your own word list
  • Wrap in <p> tags to paste directly into HTML
  • In Replace mode, click Restore to get your original text back
