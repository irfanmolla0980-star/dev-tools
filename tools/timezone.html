📖 About This Tool

What is it?

A powerful, all-in-one Time Zone Converter built for developers and remote teams. It converts time across 70+ zones, shows live seconds, handles DST, and even helps you plan meetings.

Why is it useful for developers?

  • Meeting Planner: Check the perfect overlapping hours for your global team (Work/Off indicators).
  • ISO 8601 Copy: Instantly copy the exact timestamp (e.g., 2026-10-15T14:30:00Z) for your code, API, or database.
  • URL Sharing: Share a link with your team so they see the exact same conversion you do.
  • Timestamp Converter: Convert Unix timestamps to human-readable dates instantly.
  • Country Grouping: Only see separate cities if their time zones actually differ (e.g., USA, China).

Key Features

  • 🔍 Live search by country, city, or code (e.g., "IST").
  • ⏱️ Live local clock and UTC clock with seconds.
  • 🔗 Custom shareable links for any conversion.
  • 📊 Details panel showing UTC offsets, abbreviations, and DST status.
  • 🌙 Dark / Light mode toggle.

How to use?

  1. Pick a date and time (or click Now).
  2. Choose From and To zones.
  3. Check the result, or click Copy as ISO 8601 for coding.
  4. Scroll down to the Meeting Planner to find overlapping work hours for your team.
  5. Click Share This Conversion to send the link to a colleague.

Pro Tip for Developers

Always store timestamps in UTC (using the ISO 8601 button) in your database, and let the frontend convert it to the user's local time zone. This prevents DST bugs and timezone chaos!
