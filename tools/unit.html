📖 About This Tool

What is it?

Unit Converter is a tool that converts a value from one unit to every other unit in the same category — instantly.

Categories Supported

  • 📏 Length — m, km, cm, mm, µm, nm, mile, yard, foot, inch
  • ⚖️ Weight — kg, g, mg, µg, ton, pound, ounce, stone
  • 🌡️ Temperature — Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin
  • 📐 Area — m², km², cm², ft², in², acre, hectare
  • 🧊 Volume — liter, ml, m³, gallon, pint, cup, fl oz
  • ⏱️ Time — ns, µs, ms, s, min, hour, day, week, year
  • 🚀 Speed — m/s, km/h, mph, knot, ft/s
  • 💾 Data — bit, byte, KB, MB, GB, TB, PB
  • 🔋 Energy — joule, calorie, kWh, BTU, eV
  • 📊 Pressure — pascal, bar, atm, psi, mmHg

How does it work?

Every unit is converted to a base unit first (meter, kilogram, second, etc.), then to your target unit. This ensures perfect accuracy across all conversions.

How to use?

  1. Pick a category (Length, Weight, etc.)
  2. Type a value
  3. Choose From and To units
  4. The result updates instantly
  5. See all other units at once below
  6. Click ⇅ Swap to flip units

Examples

  • 1 meter = 100 cm = 1000 mm = 3.28 ft = 39.37 in
  • 1 kg = 1000 g = 2.205 lb = 35.27 oz
  • 100 °C = 212 °F = 373.15 K
  • 1 GB = 1024 MB = 1048576 KB

Tips

  • All units in the category are shown at once
  • Click any value to copy it
  • Use ⇅ Swap to reverse the conversion
  • Results are rounded to 6 significant digits
